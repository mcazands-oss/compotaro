Privacy Policy

How Landscape Investments, LLC collects and uses your information

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

Who We Are

Landscape Investments, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is a fence, deck, and arbor contractor based in Corinth, Texas, serving the greater Denton, Dallas, and Fort Worth area. Our website is located at fenceinvestments.com.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at:

  • Phone: 940-231-4028
  • Address: 3609 Leeds Ct, Corinth, TX 76210

Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill out our contact or estimate request form
  • Use our Project Configurator tool
  • Call or email us directly

This information may include your name, phone number, email address, city or area, and details about your project. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to:

  • Respond to your estimate requests and inquiries
  • Contact you to schedule consultations or follow up on your project
  • Send you information directly related to your request

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We will never contact you with unsolicited offers unrelated to your request.

Third-Party Services

This website uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Formspree — We use Formspree to process contact form submissions. When you submit a form, your data is transmitted through Formspree's servers. Formspree's privacy policy is available at formspree.io.
  • Google Maps — We embed a Google Maps widget to display our location. Google may collect data when you interact with the map. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Google Fonts — We load web fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log your IP address when fonts are fetched. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics software. We do not track your behavior across websites. We do not use Google Analytics or any similar service.

Your browser may cache static assets (images, fonts, CSS) for performance — this is standard browser behavior and is not used to track you.

Data Retention

We retain the information you send us only for as long as needed to fulfill your estimate request or complete your project. If you would like your information removed from our records, please contact us by phone or in writing and we will honor your request promptly.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically, though we will not send you notice of minor changes.
