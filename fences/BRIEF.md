## Signature Feature: Project Configurator (configurator/index.html)
Multi-step form. Customer designs their project, submits contact info, Greg gets a full spec email.
Steps:
1. What are you adding? (Fence / Gate / Deck / Pergola or Arbor)
2. Size — Small / Medium / Large / Custom (text input)
3. Material — based on selection (e.g. Cedar, Vinyl, Aluminum, Wrought Iron, Chain Link)
4. Style — based on material (e.g. Privacy, Picket, Split Rail for wood fences)
5. Add-ons — checkboxes (Staining/Painting, Lighting, Custom Gate, Stamped Concrete, etc.)
6. Contact Info — Name*, Phone*, Email, Address/City, Additional Notes
Submit → Formspree (placeholder form ID) → Thank you message

Progress bar at top. Back/Next buttons. Smooth step transitions.
Gate/close button to exit back to main site.

## Design Notes
- Modern, clean, premium contractor feel — NOT rustic/old
- Dark hero, light content sections, alternating dark/light throughout
- Mobile-first, fully responsive
- Sticky header with scroll-shrink effect
- Logo animates in on page load (fade + slight upward slide)
- Sticky "Call Greg" floating button on mobile
- Google Maps embed in contact section (Corinth TX area)
- Schema markup: LocalBusiness, with name, phone, address, hours
- Google Fonts: Inter (body) + Playfair Display (headings)
- Smooth scroll, scroll-reveal animations on sections
- All placeholder images from Unsplash (wood fences, pergolas, decks)
